## 1. Executive Summary

**TrustFed-SDN** combines four layers for multi-tenant SDN intrusion detection — **(1) real OpenFlow 1.3 SDN isolation & live flow mitigation**, **(2) adaptive per-tenant record-level DP-SGD**, **(3) pairwise-masked verifiable secure aggregation with keyed linear checksums + Merkle binding + pre-unmask `robust_screening`**, and **(4) cosine similarity clustering** — on top of a lightweight **GRU** classifier (`43,554` trainable parameters on `InSDN`, `42,210` on `ASEADOS-SDN-IoT`).

Across `E1`–`E10`, the empirical evidence shows:

1. **Federated vs. Centralized & Non-IID (`E1`–`E2`):** On `InSDN`, plain FedAvg under Natural `N=3` reaches **`99.13 ± 0.06%`** Macro-F1 (centralized GRU: `99.74 ± 0.05%`, local-only: `98.94 ± 0.01%`, `+0.19 pts`, `5/5` seeds). Severe synthetic non-IID skewness (`Dirichlet α=0.1, N=10` on `ASEADOS-SDN-IoT`) degrades FedAvg convergence down to `71.43%` at Round 30.
2. **Byzantine Poisoning Defense (`E3`):** Under `20%` and `30%` malicious tenants, plain FedAvg collapses (`25.09%` F1 under `20%` scaling on `InSDN`; `99.96%` Backdoor ASR). Cosine Clustering preserves clean-equivalent Macro-F1 (`98.53 ± 0.07%` at `20%`, `98.51 ± 0.08%` at `30%` on `InSDN`, **`0.00 ± 0.01%` ASR**; `+73.44 pts` and `+78.21 pts` over FedAvg, `5/5` seeds, `p = 0.0625`).
3. **Adaptive Differential Privacy (`E4`):** At `mean ε̄ = 4.0`, allocating stricter privacy budgets to sensitive Finance flows (`ε₁ = 2.85` vs Uniform `4.00` on `InSDN`) keeps global Macro-F1 equivalent (`97.14 ± 1.08%` vs `97.16 ± 1.02%`) while lowering rare-cohort Finance MIA AUC (`54.27 ± 2.32%` vs `54.52%` Uniform and `57.31%` No-DP; full-partition MIA `51.18%`).
4. **Verifiable Secure Aggregation (`E5`):** With `Q=20` fixed-point quantization (RMSE `2.74e-07`), `SecureFedAvg` preserves `99.53 ± 0.21%` Macro-F1 on `InSDN` (`p = 0.6250` vs Float32 `99.67 ± 0.19%`), detects **`1,500 / 1,500` (`100.0%`)** tampered aggregates/submissions with **`0 / 300` (`0.0%`)** honest false alarms, and incurs only **`1.26×–1.29×`** round-trip wire bytes (`N=3..20`) with `48`-bit packed transport.
5. **Five-Rung Master Ablation Ladder (`E6`):** In the `12`-round, `N=10` (`max_train_samples=12,000`, `C=0.5`, `ε̄=4.0`) unified DP+SecAgg comparison, adding Cosine Clustering (`R3`) and `SecureAggregator(robust_screening=True)` (`R4`) causes **zero utility loss** relative to DP-only `R2` (`76.04%` clean on `InSDN`, `61.55%` on `ASEADOS`) while restoring **`100.0%` malicious rejection**, **`0.0%` honest false rejection**, and **`75.97 ± 0.29%`** Macro-F1 under all three `20%` attacks (vs `18.43%` label-flip and `21.73%` scaling on plain FedAvg `R1`, `5/5` seeds, `p = 0.0625`).
6. **Live Multi-Tenant SDN Testbed (`E7`, `E9`):** End-to-end HTTP FL + `ModelDeployController13` OVS mitigation (`Ubuntu 22.04 VM`) maintains `0.0%` intra-tenant ping loss (`18/18`), `100.0%` cross-tenant blocking (`6/6`), `+0.009 ms` OpenFlow ping delta under FL load (`67.3 MB` RSS), **`27.38 ms`** mean OVS `priority=300` drop-rule install latency with **`100.0%` (`3/3`) attacker blocking**, **`0.0%` (`3/3`) benign collateral block**, and automatic TTL rule expiry. Threshold calibration (`θ=0.98`) cuts raw domain-shifted live FBR from `64.26%` to **`2.70%` (`9/333`)**.
7. **Scalability (`E8`):** From `N=3` to `N=20` tenants, plain FedAvg Macro-F1 shifts cleanly by `< 0.52 pts` (`99.10% -> 98.67%` on `InSDN`, `82.83% -> 82.31%` on `ASEADOS`), full `SecureRobust` at `N=20` achieves **`98.68 ± 0.08%`** (`InSDN`, `993.3 ms` agg) and **`78.82 ± 4.05%`** (`ASEADOS`, `783.2 ms`), and crypto protocol latency scales linearly from `21.51 ms` (`N=3`, `1.256×` wire) to `82.80 ms` (`N=20`, `1.290×` wire).

## 5. Statistical Significance (`n=5` Paired Wilcoxon Tests)

All 33 paired comparisons are stored in [results/final/statistical_tests.csv](../results/final/statistical_tests.csv) and summarized in [tables/tab09_statistical_tests_and_targets.tex](../tables/tab09_statistical_tests_and_targets.tex).

> **Important Statistical Note on `n=5` Paired Seeds:** Using a two-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test (`zero_method="zsplit"`), with `n=5` independent paired random seeds (`0..4`), the mathematically smallest attainable two-sided `p`-value is $2 \times 2^{-5} = 0.0625$ even when **all `5 / 5` seeds win unanimously**. Therefore, every unanimous `5/5` robustness and scalability gain in `E1`, `E3`, and `E6` hits the exact `p = 0.0625` ceiling for `n=5` (`0` exceptions across all `5` random seeds). To avoid p-hacking, we keep `n=5` as preregistered and report both exact `p = 0.0625` and the unanimous `5/5` win count alongside `mean ± std` effect sizes (`+35` to `+78 pts`).

## 7. What Worked vs. What Did Not Work as Expected

### What Worked Strongly
- **Layering Cosine Clustering + `SecureAggregator(robust_screening=True)` on top of DP-SGD:** Pre-unmask screening of quantized per-tenant updates rejected `100.0%` of malicious updates and `0.0%` of honest updates with **0.00 pts accuracy penalty** (`R4` vs `R2`).
- **48-Bit Fixed-Point Masked Transport (`Q=20`):** Achieved Float32-equivalent intrusion detection (`p = 0.6250`) while bounding total round-trip wire overhead strictly within `1.256×–1.290×` across `N=3..20` tenants.
- **Off-Event-Loop Controller Mitigation (`ModelDeployController13`):** Putting GRU inference and HTTP polling off the OS-Ken hub kept idle-to-FL control-plane ping delta at `+0.009 ms` and delivered deterministic `27.38 ms` OVS drop enforcement.

### What Did Not Work as Expected (Reported Honestly)
1. **Zero-Shot Sim-to-Live Domain Transfer:** Models trained strictly on offline `InSDN` suffered severe benign false-block rates (`64.60%` zero-shot FBR, `64.26%` raw live FL FBR at threshold `0.5`) when exposed to live Linux VM flow statistics. Threshold calibration (`θ=0.98`, reaching `2.70%` FBR) or live adaptation (`75.46%` F1) is mandatory in deployment.
2. **DP-SGD Clean Utility in Short Low-Sample Rounds (`E6`):** While `30`-round DP at `N=3` reaches `97.14%` (`E4`), `12`-round `N=10` federated DP-SGD with `C=0.5` drops clean Macro-F1 from `96.26%` to `76.04%` (`InSDN`) and `71.42%` to `61.55%` (`ASEADOS`).
3. **ASEADOS `20%` Backdoor Seed Variance (`E3`) & Rare InSDN Trigger Overlap (`E6`):** In `E3`, Cosine Clustering achieves `74.81%` F1 on ASEADOS but experiences `59.88 ± 48.81%` backdoor ASR variance across seeds; similarly `InSDN` `R3/R4` shows `17.98 ± 32.91%` backdoor ASR on clean+overlap splits despite `100%` malicious tenant exclusion.
4. **CICIDS2017 Global Unpersonalized FedAvg:** Global FedAvg (`90.50%`) trails local-only tenant training (`95.82%`) under natural client skew because heterogeneous day/attack profiles pull shared weights away from dominant tenant traffic.
5. **Under-Trimming (`β=0.1` at `20%` Poisoning):** `TrimmedMean(β=0.1)` collapses to `38.49%` F1 because `1` malicious coordinate out of `2` remains in each `10`-tenant extreme, proving `β ≥ malicious_fraction` is strictly necessary.

## 8. Limitations, Threats to Validity & Claim Boundaries

### Explicit Limitations & Threats to Validity
- **L1 Namespace vs. L3 Full Multi-VM Tenants:** Our live SDN testbed runs multi-tenant L1 Linux network namespaces inside a **single Ubuntu 22.04 VMware VM** with per-tenant OS-Ken controllers (`c1, c2, c3`), rather than 9 separate heavyweight L3 tenant VMs.
- **No Raw Packet Payload Inspection / Encrypted Payload:** Feature extraction uses stateless L3/L4 OpenFlow/packet metadata (`19` features) and does not inspect TLS payloads.
- **Sample Size (`n=5` Seeds) & Aggregator Coverage (`ASEADOS Coord. Median`):** Preregistered 5-seed runs limit paired Wilcoxon significance to `p ≥ 0.0625`; additionally, `Coord. Median` was omitted on ASEADOS in `Step 04` and is reported as `---` in `tab03`.
- **Single-Malicious Fraction in `E6` (`20%`):** `E6` fixes poisoning at `20%` (while `E3` covers `10%–30%`); by construction, majority clustering cannot defend when malicious tenants exceed honest cluster cohesion (`≥ 50%`).
- **Linear Checksum Trust Model:** As documented in `docs/VERIFY_DESIGN.md`, keyed linear checksums verify additive arithmetic over accepted masked shares against server tampering/substitution/replay assuming tenant-held HMAC/seed confidentiality and honest-majority pre-unmask `robust_screening`.

### Safe vs. Unsupported Claims (Paper & Poster Guardrails)

| Safe Claim (Supported by `results/`) | Unsupported Claim (Do NOT Make) |
